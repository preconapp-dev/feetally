How Braintree fees work

Braintree is PayPal's developer-focused processor. It is the engine behind checkout for many apps, marketplaces and subscription businesses that want one integration for cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal and Venmo. Unlike a standard PayPal business account, Braintree gives you a full merchant account and gateway, but there is still no monthly or setup fee on standard pricing.

The standard rate for cards and digital wallets is 2.89% + 29¢ per transaction. Venmo payments through Braintree cost 3.49% + 49¢, and PayPal payments are billed at PayPal's normal merchant rates, which is 3.49% + 49¢ for PayPal Checkout. ACH Direct Debit is 0.75%, capped at $5.00 per transaction, or 1.5% + 10¢ if you want same-day processing.

Two 1% surcharges can apply to card payments: one when the card was issued outside the US, and one when the transaction is in a currency other than USD. Chargebacks cost $15. Verified 501(c)(3) charities pay 2.19% + 29¢ on cards, and established businesses with volume can ask for custom flat rates or interchange-plus pricing.

  • Cards and digital wallets (Visa, Mastercard, Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay): 2.89% + 29¢ per transaction. On $100 that is $3.18.
  • Venmo: 3.49% + 49¢ per transaction.
  • PayPal through Braintree: PayPal's merchant fees apply. PayPal Checkout is 3.49% + 49¢ per domestic transaction.
  • ACH Direct Debit: 0.75% capped at $5.00 per transaction for standard processing, or 1.5% + 10¢ for same-day processing. ACH returns and disputes cost $5.00.
  • International cards: an additional 1% on cards issued outside the United States. Non-USD currency: an additional 1% when the transaction is not in US dollars.
  • American Express pass-through: if you hold your own Amex merchant agreement, Braintree charges 15¢ per Amex transaction with no percentage.
  • Chargebacks: $15.00 each. Optional Chargeback Protection starts at 0.4% per transaction and Fraud Protection Lite costs 5¢ per inquiry.

Ways to keep more of each Braintree sale

  • Push large B2B payments to ACH. The $5 cap means a $5,000 invoice costs $5 instead of $144.79 by card.
  • Bill in USD. Presenting prices in another currency adds 1%, and a foreign-issued card adds another 1%.
  • Once you are processing serious volume, ask Braintree about interchange-plus pricing; the fee page says custom flat rates and interchange-plus are available based on volume.
  • Steer customers toward cards or wallets rather than Venmo or PayPal at checkout when you can; the difference is 0.6 points plus 20¢ on every order.
  • If you already have an Amex agreement, use pass-through: 15¢ per Amex transaction is far less than 2.89% + 29¢.

Braintree fee FAQ

How much does Braintree take from $100?

$3.18 for a card or digital-wallet payment (2.89% + 29¢), leaving $96.82. A Venmo or PayPal payment costs $3.98.

Does Braintree charge a monthly fee?

No. Standard Braintree pricing lists no monthly, setup or gateway fees; you pay per transaction.

What is the Braintree rate for PayPal payments?

PayPal transactions through Braintree are charged at PayPal's merchant fees, which is 3.49% + 49¢ for PayPal Checkout in the US.

Is Braintree cheaper than Stripe?

Slightly, at published rates: 2.89% + 29¢ versus Stripe's 2.9% + 30¢, about 2¢ less on a $100 sale. Braintree's international surcharge is 1% versus Stripe's 1.5%.

How much is Braintree's ACH fee?

0.75% of the payment, capped at $5.00. Same-day ACH is 1.5% + 10¢.

What does Braintree charge for a chargeback?

$15.00 per chargeback. ACH returns or disputes cost $5.00.
