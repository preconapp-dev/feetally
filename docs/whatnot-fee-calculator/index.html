How Whatnot fees work

Whatnot is a live-shopping marketplace where sellers run auctions and buy-it-now sales on video, plus a standard marketplace for fixed-price listings. Sports cards, trading card games, coins, collectibles, and fashion are the biggest categories, and sellers range from hobbyists streaming on weekends to full-time card shops.

Since September 21, 2026, Whatnot's commission depends on your category and your total sales over the previous 28 days. The standard rate, for anyone under $15,000 in that window, is 8% of the item sale price in every category except coins, which is 4%. Above $15,000 the rate steps down through six tiers: sports and TCG bottom out at 6.5% past $500,000, fashion at 4.5%, other collectibles at 5%, coins at 3.5%, and everything else at 4%. Your rate is locked for each four-week period, and cancelled orders are subtracted from your total.

Every order also pays a 2.9% + $0.30 payment processing fee, charged on the full amount the buyer pays, including shipping and sales tax. Commission itself is charged on the item price only.

For a $50 card with $6 buyer-paid shipping at the standard tier, that works out to $4.00 commission and $1.92 processing, leaving $50.08 before your label and product cost.

  • Commission, standard tier (under $15,000 in sales over the 28-day period): 8% of the item sale price in sports, TCG, fashion, other collectibles, and all other categories; 4% for coins and money.
  • Volume tiers: your combined sales across all categories, live shows and marketplace, over a four-week (28-day) period set your tier for the next period. Tier 1 starts at $15,000, Tier 2 at $35,000, Tier 3 at $50,000, Tier 4 at $100,000, Tier 5 at $250,000, and Tier 6 at $500,000.
  • Tiered rates by category: sports and TCG go 7.75%, 7.5%, 7.25%, 7%, 6.75%, 6.5% from Tier 1 to Tier 6; fashion 7%, 6.5%, 5.5%, 5%, 4.5%, 4.5%; other collectibles 7.25%, 6.5%, 6%, 5%, 5%, 5%; coins 3.9%, 3.8%, 3.7%, 3.6%, 3.55%, 3.5%; everything else 7%, 6.5%, 5.5%, 4%, 4%, 4%.
  • What commission is charged on: the item sale price (gross merchandise value). Shipping and taxes paid by the buyer are not included.
  • Payment processing: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction in the US, calculated on the total order value the buyer pays, including item price, buyer-paid shipping, and taxes.
  • Cancelled orders are subtracted from your sales total for the period they were placed in, so they do not help you reach a tier.

Ways to keep more of each Whatnot sale

  • Watch the $0.30 on cheap lots. A $2 card with $4 shipping pays $0.16 commission but $0.47 processing, about a quarter of the item price. Bundle low-value cards into bigger lots.
  • Coins are the cheapest category to sell at 4% standard. Do not tag coin lots as 'other collectibles' and pay 8%.
  • If you are close to $15,000 in a 28-day window, a strong final show can lift the whole next four weeks to Tier 1: 7.75% instead of 8% on sports and TCG, or 7% on fashion.
  • Buyer-paid shipping is not fee-free. It escapes commission but still pays 2.9% processing, so on a $6 label the fee is about $0.17.
  • Price for the combined bite: at the standard tier, roughly 10.9% of the item price plus $0.30 and 2.9% of shipping leaves your account on every sports-card sale.

Whatnot fee FAQ

What are Whatnot's seller fees in 2026?

An 8% commission on the item sale price for most categories (4% for coins), reduced in tiers once your 28-day sales exceed $15,000, plus 2.9% + $0.30 payment processing on the total order value.

Does Whatnot charge commission on shipping?

No. Commission is calculated on the item price only. The 2.9% + $0.30 processing fee, however, is charged on the full order total including buyer-paid shipping and taxes.

How much does Whatnot take on a $100 sale?

At the standard tier with $8 buyer-paid shipping: $8.00 commission plus 2.9% of $108 + $0.30 = $3.43 processing, so $11.43 in fees and a payout of $96.57 before your label cost.

How do Whatnot's volume tiers work?

Your total completed sales across every category and format over a 28-day period set your commission tier for the next 28-day period. Under $15,000 is the standard rate; Tier 1 starts at $15,000 and Tier 6 at $500,000. The rate is locked for the full period.

Did Whatnot change its fees?

Yes. A new tiered commission structure took effect September 21, 2026 for existing sellers. Sellers who joined after that date start on the new structure the following Monday.

Does Whatnot charge a fee on canceled orders?

Cancelled orders are subtracted from your sales total for the period. Refund and cancellation fee handling is covered in Whatnot's help center; it is not modeled here.

Is there a listing or monthly fee on Whatnot?

No. Whatnot's published schedule is commission plus payment processing; there is no listing fee or subscription.

Good to know

The commission table and processing fee are from Whatnot's US fee schedule as updated for the September 21, 2026 tier structure. Processing is charged on taxes the buyer pays; this calculator does not include tax, so the processing line is slightly low on taxable orders (add 2.9% of the tax). Whatnot runs periodic reduced-commission promotions (for example on electronics, coins, or high-value orders) that are not modeled. Shipping-label pricing, giveaway shipping, and payout timing were not verified on the fee schedule page. Whether a tiered rate is also applied to sellers with the seller-level promotions active is not stated.
