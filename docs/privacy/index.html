Privacy policy

Last updated Sep 30, 2026.

The short version

FeeTally does not ask you to create an account and does not collect the numbers you type into a calculator; calculations run in your browser and are not sent to us. We use standard analytics and advertising services that may set cookies. You can opt out of personalized advertising, and visitors in some regions will see a consent prompt first.

Information we collect

Calculator inputs. Values you enter stay in your browser. If you use "Copy link to this calculation", the values are placed in the page URL so you can share it; we don't store that link.

Usage data. Like most websites, our hosting provider and analytics service record standard technical information: pages visited, approximate location derived from IP address, browser type, referring site, and time on page. We use this to understand which calculators are useful and to keep the site working.

Email. If you email us, we keep the message to respond to it and to fix the issue you reported.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

We may use Google Analytics to measure traffic. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar identifiers; Google's privacy policy explains how it handles data: policies.google.com/privacy.

This site is supported by advertising served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will see a consent message before advertising cookies are set, and you can change your choice at any time from the link in the footer of that message.

Your choices

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the calculators work without cookies.
  • Use the opt-out links above for personalized advertising.
  • Email us to ask what data, if any, we hold about you, or to have it deleted.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We'll update this page if our practices change and revise the date at the top.
