How Indiegogo fees work

Indiegogo is the second major rewards crowdfunding platform in the United States, with a long history in consumer hardware and gadgets. On October 16, 2025 it migrated to a new platform built on Gamefound technology, and several rules changed with it: flexible "keep what you raise" funding is no longer available for new campaigns, end dates cannot be changed once a campaign is live, and the post-campaign phase formerly called InDemand is now called Late Pledge.

The fees were simplified at the same time. Indiegogo charges a flat 5% platform fee on the collected amount, plus a payment processing fee of 3% plus $0.20 per transaction. Indiegogo's own example: a campaign that raises $10,000 across 100 transactions pays $500 in platform fee and $320 in processing, and $9,180 lands in the bank account. If the campaign does not reach its goal, you pay nothing, including payment processing fees.

The pledge manager, which collects add-ons, shipping and late pledges after the campaign, costs nothing to use; Indiegogo takes the same 5% of each successfully collected payment plus 3% + $0.20 per transaction. Campaigns launched after the upgrade are paid out through Adyen, while campaigns that were live before October 16, 2025 stay on Stripe.

  • Platform fee: 5% of the collected amount, charged on a successfully funded campaign.
  • Payment processing: 3% of the collected amount plus $0.20 for every transaction, in the project currency. Fifty backers who each pay $200 cost $10 in fixed fees; 500 backers paying $20 each cost $100 on the same total.
  • Unfunded campaigns: if your project does not reach its goal you pay nothing, including payment processing fees. New campaigns are fixed funding only.
  • Pledge manager: no separate charge; Indiegogo takes 5% of each successfully collected payment plus 3% + $0.20 per transaction on add-ons, shipping and late pledges.
  • No upfront costs: launching a campaign is free; fees are deducted from the collected funds before payout.
  • Processor: campaigns created after October 16, 2025 process payments through Adyen; older campaigns remain on Stripe.

Ways to keep more of each Indiegogo sale

  • Plan on about 8%–9% in fees at typical perk prices. Add-ons collected later through the pledge manager cost the same 8% + $0.20 per transaction.
  • Because new campaigns are fixed funding, set a goal you will clear; a campaign that misses pays nothing but also collects nothing.
  • Consolidate shipping and add-ons into a backer's single pledge-manager checkout where possible, since every transaction costs $0.20.
  • Price perks so the 5% + 3% + $0.20 and shipping still leave your margin; the calculator's "amount you need to keep" field works backward from your required net.
  • Remember the end date is locked once you launch on the new platform, so build your fulfillment costs and fee math before going live.

Indiegogo fee FAQ

What fees does Indiegogo charge?

A 5% platform fee on the collected amount plus payment processing of 3% + $0.20 per transaction. That is about 8.2% on a $10,000 campaign with 100 contributions.

Does Indiegogo charge if the campaign fails?

No. Indiegogo says that if your project doesn't reach its goal you won't pay anything, including payment processing fees.

How much does Indiegogo take on $10,000?

$500 in platform fee plus $300 in processing plus $0.20 per transaction. With 100 contributions that is $820, leaving $9,180, which is Indiegogo's own example.

Does Indiegogo still offer flexible funding?

No. Since the October 16, 2025 platform upgrade, flexible funding is not available for new campaigns; live flexible campaigns were converted to fixed funding.

What does the Indiegogo pledge manager cost?

Nothing to use. Indiegogo takes 5% of each successfully collected payment plus 3% + $0.20 per transaction, the same as the campaign.

What happened to InDemand?

It was renamed Late Pledge in the 2025 upgrade. It is the post-campaign funding phase and is charged the same 5% + processing.
