Gig, hosting, events and crowdfunding fee calculators

Airbnb, Vrbo, Turo, Rover, DoorDash, Uber Eats, Eventbrite, Kickstarter and GoFundMe.

Airbnb fee calculatorAirbnb charges hosts either a 3% split fee (guests then pay a 14.1%–16.5% service fee on top) or a 15.5% host-only fee with no guest service fee. Both are calculated on the booking subtotal: nightly price plus cleaning and other host fees, before taxes. DoorDash (Merchants) fee calculatorDoorDash Marketplace charges restaurants a commission on each order's subtotal: 15% on Basic, 25% on Plus and 30% on Premier for delivery orders, and 6% on pickup orders on every plan. There are no activation, subscription or software fees. Eventbrite fee calculatorEventbrite charges a service fee of 3.7% + $1.79 per paid ticket and a payment processing fee of 2.9% of the order. Free tickets carry no fees. Attendees pay the fees by default, or you can absorb them and have them deducted from your payout. GoFundMe fee calculatorGoFundMe charges no platform fee to start or run a fundraiser. Each donation carries a transaction fee of 2.9% + $0.30 (2.2% + $0.30 for certified nonprofits), deducted automatically. Donors may add an optional tip to GoFundMe, which does not affect your total. Indiegogo fee calculatorIndiegogo charges a flat 5% platform fee on the amount collected plus payment processing of 3% + $0.20 per transaction. If a campaign does not reach its goal, nothing is charged, including processing fees. Kickstarter fee calculatorIf your project is funded, Kickstarter keeps 5% of the total raised plus payment processing of 3% + $0.30 per pledge (5% + $0.08 for pledges under $10). If the project is not funded, there are no fees. Rover fee calculatorRover takes a 20% service fee from sitters and dog walkers on each booking, so you keep 80% of your rates. Pet owners separately pay an 11% booking fee at checkout. Some US pilot markets use 30%/15%/10% tiers based on your history with each client, and California owners pay a 25% marketplace fee instead of the sitter being charged. Turo fee calculatorTuro hosts keep 60%, 75%, 80%, 85% or 90% of the trip price depending on the earnings plan they choose; Turo keeps the remaining 40% to 10%. Higher-share plans come with less physical damage protection and higher deductibles. Uber Eats (Merchants) fee calculatorUber Eats charges restaurants a marketplace fee per delivery order of 20% on Lite (as low as 15% in select cities), 25% on Plus, 30% on Premium and 15% on the Self-delivery plan. Pickup orders are 7% with validated in-store pricing, otherwise 10%. Vrbo fee calculatorVrbo's pay-per-booking model charges a flat 12% commission on the rental amount and any fees you charge travelers, plus a 3% payment processing fee on the total payment including taxes and refundable deposits. The commission was 5% before Vrbo's 2026 change.
