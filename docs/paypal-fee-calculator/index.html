How PayPal fees work

PayPal is the most widely recognized way to get paid online, and a business account lets you take PayPal, Venmo, credit and debit cards, invoices and in-person QR code payments. The catch is that the fee depends heavily on which of those products the money comes through.

The standard PayPal Checkout rate for a US business is 3.49% + 49¢ per domestic transaction. That covers the PayPal button, guest checkout and Pay with Venmo. Cards run through PayPal's standard card fields cost 2.99% + 49¢, and Advanced Credit and Debit Card Payments cost 2.89% + 29¢. QR code payments in person are the cheapest at 2.29% + 9¢. If a customer pays you through the PayPal app and marks it as goods and services, the fee is a flat 2.99% with no fixed part.

Two surcharges stack on top: an extra 1.50% when the payer is outside the US, and a 4.00% currency conversion spread when PayPal converts the payment. Chargebacks cost $20 and standard disputes $15. There are no monthly fees for a standard business account. Micropayments pricing (4.99% + 9¢) is a separate plan you have to request, and it only pays off on sales under about $10.

  • PayPal Checkout, guest checkout and Pay with Venmo: 3.49% + 49¢ per domestic transaction. On $100 that is $3.98.
  • Standard credit and debit card payments: 2.99% + 49¢. Advanced Credit and Debit Card Payments (PayPal's hosted card fields, subject to approval): 2.89% + 29¢.
  • QR code payments in person: 2.29% + 9¢ per transaction.
  • Send/Receive Money for Goods and Services (a customer pays you in the app and marks it as a purchase): 2.99% with no fixed fee.
  • Invoices: 3.49% + 49¢ when the customer pays with PayPal or Venmo, 2.99% + 49¢ when they pay by card.
  • Micropayments: 4.99% + 9¢, a separate pricing plan you must request. PayPal Pay Later (Pay in 4, Pay Monthly): 4.99% + 49¢.
  • International transactions add 1.50%. Currency conversion adds 4.00% on a goods-and-services payment (3.00% on other conversions, such as converting a balance). Chargeback fee $20, standard dispute fee $15, high-volume dispute fee $30.

Ways to keep more of each PayPal sale

  • Route card payments through the standard or advanced card products instead of the PayPal button where you can: 2.99% + 49¢ or 2.89% + 29¢ beats 3.49% + 49¢.
  • Use QR codes for in-person sales. At 2.29% + 9¢ it is the cheapest PayPal rate, less than half a point above Zettle's reader rate.
  • Ask for micropayments pricing only if most sales are under $10. On a $5 sale it costs 34¢ instead of 66¢, but on a $50 sale it costs $2.59 instead of $2.24.
  • Hold and get paid in USD. If a customer pays in another currency, the 4.00% conversion spread plus 1.50% international fee take almost 9% of a PayPal Checkout sale.
  • Skip instant transfers unless you need the money today. Standard transfers to your bank are free; instant ones cost 1.50% with a 50¢ minimum.

PayPal fee FAQ

How much does PayPal take from $100?

$3.98 for a standard PayPal Checkout payment (3.49% + 49¢), so you keep $96.02. A card payment through standard card processing costs $3.48, and a goods-and-services payment sent in the app costs $2.99.

What is the PayPal fee for goods and services?

2.99% with no fixed fee when someone pays you in the PayPal app and marks the payment as goods and services. Payments through a PayPal Checkout button on a website are 3.49% + 49¢.

Does PayPal charge a monthly fee for a business account?

No. A standard PayPal business account has no monthly fee; you pay per transaction.

What is PayPal's international fee?

An additional 1.50% on any commercial transaction where the payer is outside the US, plus a 4.00% spread if PayPal converts the currency.

How much is a PayPal chargeback fee?

$20 for a chargeback filed through the card issuer, $15 for a standard dispute filed through PayPal, or $30 per dispute if your account is in the high-volume dispute tier.

What is the PayPal fee on a $1,000 invoice?

$35.39 if the client pays with PayPal or Venmo (3.49% + 49¢), or $30.39 if they pay by card (2.99% + 49¢).

How much does PayPal charge for QR code payments?

2.29% + 9¢ per in-person QR code transaction, whether the customer pays with PayPal or Venmo.
