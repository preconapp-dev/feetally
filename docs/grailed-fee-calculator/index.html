How Grailed fees work

Grailed is a peer-to-peer marketplace for menswear, streetwear, designer, and vintage clothing, sneakers, and accessories. Sellers are mostly individual collectors and resellers moving mid- to high-priced pieces, and buyers negotiate through offers before checkout.

There are two seller fees. The commission depends on the sale price: 9% on sales of $120 or more, and 6% with a $1.99 minimum on sales under $120 (the reduced tier applies to sales on or after May 20, 2026). If you ship with a Grailed Label, the commission is calculated on the listing price only; if you ship on your own, the shipping you charge the buyer is included in the base.

On top of that, Grailed Payments (run on Stripe) charges a payment processing fee: 3.49% + $0.49 when the buyer is in the US, or 4.99% + $0.49 for an international buyer, for sellers who have completed Stripe onboarding. Sellers in a Stripe-supported country who have not finished onboarding pay a higher $0.99 fixed fee.

Buyers pay nothing extra to Grailed. On a typical $150 sale with $10 shipping, a US seller pays $19.57 in total and receives $140.43, released to their bank account within 3 calendar days of delivery.

  • Commission, $120 and above: 9% of the sale price. On a $150 sale that is $13.50.
  • Commission, under $120: 6% of the sale price with a $1.99 minimum, for sales on or after May 20, 2026. On an $80 sale that is $4.80; on a $25 sale the $1.99 minimum applies.
  • What counts as the sale price: the listing price your item sells for, plus any shipping you charge the buyer if you do not use a Grailed Label. With a Grailed Label, only the listing price counts.
  • Payment processing (Grailed Payments via Stripe): 3.49% + $0.49 per sale for a US buyer, 4.99% + $0.49 for an international buyer. This calculator applies it to the item price plus shipping, which is what the buyer is charged.
  • Not yet onboarded with Stripe: the fixed part rises to $0.99 (3.49% + $0.99 domestic, 5.49% + $0.99 international). Finish onboarding before you sell.
  • Buyers pay no Grailed fee. Sales tax is collected from the buyer by Grailed and is not part of your commission base.
  • Payout: Grailed releases funds to your bank account within 3 calendar days of the item showing as delivered, then your bank takes 2 to 3 business days.

Ways to keep more of each Grailed sale

  • Use a Grailed Label when you charge for shipping. Without it, a $150 sale with $15 shipping is commissioned on $165 instead of $150, which costs an extra $1.35.
  • Mind the $120 line. A $119 sale pays 6% ($7.14); a $121 sale pays 9% ($10.89). If an offer lands just above $120, countering slightly lower can leave you with more.
  • Finish Stripe onboarding before listing. The unonboarded rate adds $0.50 per sale on domestic orders and a full point plus $0.50 on international ones.
  • Price for offers. Grailed buyers expect to negotiate, and a $10 drop on a $150 sale only costs you about $8.75 in payout because the 9% and 3.49% shrink with it.
  • Free shipping with a higher price and free shipping with a lower price and a shipping charge are not equal: with a Grailed Label, shipping escapes the 9% commission but still pays 3.49% processing.

Grailed fee FAQ

What are Grailed's seller fees in 2026?

9% commission on sales of $120 or more, 6% (minimum $1.99) on sales under $120, plus payment processing of 3.49% + $0.49 for US buyers or 4.99% + $0.49 for international buyers.

Does Grailed charge commission on shipping?

Only if you ship without a Grailed Label. With a Grailed Label, commission is calculated on the listing price alone; otherwise the shipping you charge the buyer is included.

Does Grailed still use PayPal?

Grailed's current fee page lists only Grailed Payments, which runs on Stripe, with payouts to a bank account. PayPal is not mentioned as a payment or payout option on the official fee pages.

How much does Grailed take on a $200 sale?

With $10 shipping and a Grailed Label: 9% of $200 = $18.00 commission plus 3.49% of $210 + $0.49 = $7.82 processing, so $25.82 in fees and a $184.18 payout.

When does Grailed pay sellers?

Funds are released to your bank account within 3 calendar days of the tracking showing delivered. US banks then take 2 to 3 business days to post it.

Are there Grailed fees for buyers?

No. Grailed is free for buyers; the buyer pays the item price, shipping, and any sales tax.

Does Grailed charge a fee if my item doesn't sell?

No. Listing is free and both the commission and processing fee are charged only when an item sells.

Good to know

The 6% / $1.99-minimum tier for sales under $120 is stated on Grailed's fee page as applicable to sales on or after May 20, 2026; sales before that date were charged 9% across the board. Grailed's processing-fee page does not state whether the 3.49% + $0.49 is calculated on the item price alone or on the item price plus shipping; this calculator uses item price plus shipping (the amount the buyer is charged), so the processing line may be a few cents high if Grailed excludes shipping. Rates shown are for sellers onboarded with Grailed Payments (Stripe); unonboarded sellers pay $0.99 fixed instead of $0.49. Grailed reserves the right to modify all fees at any time.
