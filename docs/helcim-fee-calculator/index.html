How Helcim fees work

Helcim is a payment processor for small and mid-sized businesses that prices every card transaction as interchange plus. Instead of one flat rate, you pay the wholesale cost of the specific card, which is the interchange set for that card type plus the card network's own fee, and Helcim adds a fixed margin on top. Helcim's pricing page lists no account monthly fee, no signup fee, no PCI fee and no cancellation fee.

The margin depends on how the card is taken and on your volume. In person (tap, chip or swipe) it is 0.40% + 8¢ per transaction at up to $50,000 a month; keyed and online payments are 0.50% + 25¢. Both drop automatically as your average monthly card volume over the last three months grows: 0.35% + 7¢ and 0.45% + 20¢ from $50,000, then down in steps to 0.15% + 6¢ and 0.15% + 15¢ at $1 million or more.

Interchange varies by card. Helcim's Visa table lists a basic consumer credit card at 1.51% + 10¢ when the card is present and 1.90% + 10¢ when it is keyed, with rewards cards higher and regulated debit at 0.05% + 22¢. A $100 in-person sale on that basic credit card costs $1.61 in interchange, $0.14 in Visa's card-brand fee and $0.48 to Helcim, $2.23 in all. Helcim says that for most merchants the total in-person effective rate comes out near or below 2%.

  • Interchange: passed through at cost and different for every card type. Helcim's Visa table lists CPS Retail consumer credit at 1.51% + 10¢, Rewards Traditional at 1.65% + 10¢, Signature Preferred and Infinite at 2.10% + 10¢, swiped consumer debit at 0.80% + 15¢ and regulated debit at 0.05% + 22¢. Keyed versions cost more: 1.90%, 2.05% and 2.52% + 10¢ for the three credit categories.
  • Card-brand fee: the card network's own fee is also passed through. Helcim lists Visa's at 0.14% of the transaction.
  • Helcim margin, in person: 0.40% + 8¢ at $0 to $50K a month, 0.35% + 7¢ at $50K to $100K, 0.25% + 7¢ at $100K to $500K, 0.20% + 6¢ at $500K to $1M, and 0.15% + 6¢ at $1M to $5M.
  • Helcim margin, keyed and online: 0.50% + 25¢ at $0 to $50K a month, 0.45% + 20¢ at $50K to $100K, 0.35% + 20¢ at $100K to $500K, 0.25% + 15¢ at $500K to $1M, and 0.15% + 15¢ at $1M to $5M.
  • Volume tiers: your tier is set by your average monthly credit card processing volume over the last three months and adjusts automatically each month.
  • Recurring payments add 0.4% per transaction. ACH and EFT payments cost 0.5% + 25¢ per transaction, capped at $6; a rejected ACH payment costs $5.
  • Other costs: chargebacks are $15, and $0 for cases resolved in your favor. There are no monthly, signup, PCI or cancellation fees. Hardware is optional: the Helcim Card Reader is $199 and the Smart Terminal is $349 or $32 a month for a year, plus shipping.

Ways to keep more of each Helcim sale

  • Take the card in person when you can. A $100 basic Visa credit sale costs $2.23 tapped but $2.89 keyed, because both interchange (1.90% against 1.51%) and Helcim's margin (0.50% + 25¢ against 0.40% + 8¢) are higher.
  • Debit is far cheaper than credit. Regulated Visa debit is 0.05% + 22¢ in interchange, so a $100 in-person sale costs about $0.89 in total at the first tier.
  • Send large invoices by ACH. At 0.5% + 25¢ capped at $6, a $5,000 invoice costs $6 instead of about $127 on a keyed basic credit card.
  • Dispute chargebacks you can win. The $15 fee is $0 for cases resolved in your favor.
  • Check your tier before comparing quotes. The margin falls on its own at $50,000, $100,000, $500,000 and $1 million in average monthly volume, with no application needed.

Helcim fee FAQ

How much does Helcim charge per transaction?

Interchange plus Helcim's margin. At up to $50,000 a month the margin is 0.40% + 8¢ in person and 0.50% + 25¢ keyed or online. On a $100 tap with a basic Visa credit card (1.51% + 10¢ interchange and a 0.14% card-brand fee) the total is $2.23.

Does Helcim charge a monthly fee?

No. Helcim's pricing page lists no account monthly fee, no signup fee, a $0 PCI fee and no cancellation fee. An optional 4G wireless plan for hardware is $7 a month.

What is interchange plus pricing?

You pay the wholesale cost of each card, which is the interchange paid to the card-issuing bank plus card-network fees, and the processor adds a fixed, published margin. With Helcim that margin is the only part Helcim keeps.

How do Helcim's volume discounts work?

Your margin tier is based on your average monthly credit card processing volume over the last three months and updates automatically. The in-person margin falls from 0.40% + 8¢ under $50,000 to 0.15% + 6¢ at $1 million or more.

Is Helcim cheaper than Stripe?

It depends on the card. On a $100 online sale with a basic Visa credit card, Helcim's total at the first tier is about $2.89 against Stripe's published 2.9% + 30¢ ($3.20). A premium rewards card keyed at 2.52% + 10¢ interchange brings Helcim's total to about $3.51.

What does Helcim charge for ACH payments?

0.5% + 25¢ per transaction, capped at $6. A rejected ACH payment costs $5.

How much is a Helcim chargeback fee?

$15 per chargeback, and $0 for cases resolved in your favor.

Good to know

Helcim's margins are from its pricing page and its volume-discount help article (dated September 26, 2025). Helcim does not publish an average effective rate beyond saying most merchants' in-person total is near or below 2%, so this calculator asks for the interchange rate instead of assuming one. The defaults (1.51% + 10¢ interchange and a 0.14% card-brand fee) are Visa's CPS Retail consumer credit figures from Helcim's Visa interchange table and are only an example; Mastercard, Discover and other card types have their own rates. American Express, international-card and currency-conversion pricing were not listed on the pages read and are not modeled. The 0.4% recurring-payment charge, ACH payments, chargebacks and hardware are described above but not modeled. Volume above $5 million a month is outside the published tiers.
