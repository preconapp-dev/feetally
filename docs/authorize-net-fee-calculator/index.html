How Authorize.net fees work

Authorize.net is one of the oldest payment gateways in the US and is still the default choice for businesses that run a traditional merchant account, a virtual terminal or an older shopping cart. Visa owns it. The pricing is simple, but it works differently from Stripe or Square because it is split into two options.

The All-in-One option bundles a merchant account with the gateway: $25 a month plus 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction. The Payment Gateway Only option is for businesses that already have a merchant account with a bank or processor: $25 a month, 10¢ per transaction and a 10¢ daily batch fee, on top of whatever your own processor charges. eCheck (ACH) processing through the gateway costs 0.75% per transaction with a $10 monthly minimum.

There are no setup fees, no contracts and no early termination fees, and the credit-card chargeback fee is $0.00 (eCheck chargebacks are $25). Because the $25 is monthly, the per-order share depends on your volume: 100 transactions a month adds 25¢ each; 20 transactions adds $1.25 each. Ways to keep more of each Authorize.net sale

  • If you already have a competitive merchant account, use Gateway Only: 10¢ a transaction plus 10¢ a batch day is cheap for card-present or B2B businesses with large tickets.
  • If you are starting fresh with modest volume, All-in-One at 2.9% + 30¢ is on par with Stripe, but only after the $25 monthly fee is spread over enough orders. Under about 50 transactions a month it adds 50¢ or more to each sale.
  • Settle batches only on days you have sales; the 10¢ batch fee is charged per day a batch is submitted.
  • Take large invoices by eCheck at 0.75%: a $2,000 payment costs $15 instead of $58.30 by card on All-in-One.
  • Use Customer Information Manager for repeat billing. It is included free and avoids re-keying cards.

Authorize.net fee FAQ

How much does Authorize.net cost per month?

$25 a month on both the All-in-One and Payment Gateway Only options.

What is the Authorize.net transaction fee?

2.9% + 30¢ on All-in-One, or 10¢ per transaction plus a 10¢ daily batch fee on Gateway Only, where your merchant account bills its own rate on top.

How much does Authorize.net take from $100?

$3.20 on All-in-One, plus your share of the $25 monthly fee (25¢ at 100 transactions a month). On Gateway Only it is 10¢ plus your own processor's fee.

Does Authorize.net charge a setup fee or require a contract?

No. The pricing page lists no setup fee, no contract and no early termination fee.

Does Authorize.net charge for chargebacks?

Not for credit cards ($0.00). eCheck chargebacks cost $25.00.

What is the Authorize.net eCheck fee?

0.75% per eCheck transaction, with a $10 monthly minimum.

Good to know

The Gateway Only option's per-transaction cost excludes your merchant account's own processing fees (interchange plus your processor's markup), which vary. The daily batch fee is modeled as 10¢ multiplied by the number of batch days you enter, spread across your monthly transactions. The eCheck $10 monthly minimum is not modeled. No international surcharge is listed on the pricing page. Payout timing is not stated on the pricing page.
