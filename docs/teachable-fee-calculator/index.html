How Teachable fees work

Teachable is a hosted platform for selling online courses, coaching and digital downloads. You build the course pages, Teachable handles checkout, hosting, student accounts and payouts, and you pay a monthly plan plus per-sale fees.

There is no free plan: Teachable's pricing page says it is a paid service with a 7-day free trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Three plans are listed. Starter is $39 a month, or $29 a month billed annually, and takes a 7.5% transaction fee on every sale. Builder is $89 a month ($69 annually) and Growth is $189 a month ($139 annually); both have a 0% transaction fee when you use teachable:pay or the Monthly Payment Gateway. A Custom plan is priced by sales. Annual billing is about 22% cheaper than monthly.

On top of the plan, every sale pays payment processing at Teachable's standard rates: 2.9% + 30¢ for US credit and debit cards, 3.9% + 30¢ for international cards, 3.49% + 49¢ for US PayPal and 4.99% + 49¢ for international PayPal, plus a $15 fee per chargeback. The calculator spreads the plan price across your monthly sales so you can see the true cost per sale and the point where Builder's $40 higher price beats Starter's 7.5%.

  • Starter plan: $39 a month billed monthly or $29 a month billed annually, plus a 7.5% transaction fee on every sale.
  • Builder plan: $89 a month billed monthly or $69 a month billed annually, with a 0% transaction fee when using teachable:pay or the Monthly Payment Gateway.
  • Growth plan: $189 a month billed monthly or $139 a month billed annually, with the same 0% transaction fee.
  • Payment processing, charged on all plans: US cards 2.9% + 30¢; international cards 3.9% + 30¢; US PayPal 3.49% + 49¢; international PayPal 4.99% + 49¢.
  • Chargebacks: $15 per chargeback.
  • Plan price per sale: the calculator divides the monthly plan price by your sales per month. At 20 sales a month, Starter annual adds $1.45 per sale and Builder annual adds $3.45.
  • Trial and refunds: 7-day free trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee on all plans. No free tier is listed.

Ways to keep more of each Teachable sale

  • Move from Starter to Builder once you sell about $533 a month on annual billing: 7.5% of $533 is $40, the price difference between the two plans.
  • Pay annually if you expect to sell for a year. Starter drops from $39 to $29 a month, saving $120, and the transaction fee is unchanged.
  • Steer students to card checkout where you can; PayPal costs 3.49% + 49¢ versus 2.9% + 30¢ on US cards, 78¢ more on a $100 sale.
  • Price to cover the flat parts. On a $20 product the 30¢ processing and the amortized plan price are a bigger share than on a $200 course.
  • Use the sales-per-month field honestly; a $139 Growth plan at 5 sales a month costs $27.80 per sale, more than the 7.5% Starter fee on most course prices.

Teachable fee FAQ

Does Teachable take a percentage of sales?

Only on the Starter plan, which charges 7.5% per sale. Builder and Growth have a 0% transaction fee. All plans pay payment processing, 2.9% + 30¢ on US cards.

How much does Teachable cost per month?

Starter is $39 a month ($29 billed annually), Builder $89 ($69 annually) and Growth $189 ($139 annually). Custom plans are quoted by sales.

Is there a free Teachable plan?

No. Teachable says it is a paid service with a 7-day free trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee. In October 2026 a pop-up on the pricing page also advertised a 30-day free trial.

What are Teachable's payment processing fees?

2.9% + 30¢ for US credit and debit cards, 3.9% + 30¢ for international cards, 3.49% + 49¢ for US PayPal and 4.99% + 49¢ for international PayPal, plus $15 per chargeback.

How much do I keep from a $100 course sale on Teachable?

$89.30 after the 7.5% fee and US card processing on Starter, before the plan price; spreading a $29 annual Starter plan over 20 monthly sales brings it to $87.85. On Builder it is $96.80 before the plan price.

When is Builder cheaper than Starter?

When 7.5% of your monthly sales exceeds the $40 difference in annual plan price, which happens at about $533 in monthly sales ($50 more per month, or $667 in sales, on monthly billing).

Good to know

Prices and fees are from Teachable's pricing page, read 2026-10-10, which lists Starter, Builder, Growth and Custom; no free plan or $1 + 10% fee appears on the page any longer. The plan cards and FAQ on that page say 7-day free trial, while a pop-up on the same page says the trial has been extended from 7 to 30 days. Teachable states the 0% transaction fee on Builder and Growth applies when using teachable:pay or the Monthly Payment Gateway; other gateway setups are not modeled. Processing rates are Teachable's standard rates and can differ if you connect your own gateway: Teachable's fees help article lists a 2% integration fee per transaction for US-based schools on a Stripe Custom Payment Gateway. The same article lists a 0.95% currency conversion fee and a 4.4% + 30¢ card rate on a line labeled 'International non-US/EU/AU school', and the pricing page lists an optional BackOffice fee of 2–2.8%; none of these are modeled. Sales tax handling and payout schedules are not modeled.
