Resale app fee calculators

Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, StockX, GOAT, Whatnot and other apps for selling clothes, sneakers and collectibles.

Depop fee calculatorUS sellers pay no Depop selling fee; the only standard deduction is a 3.3% + $0.45 payment processing fee on the item price plus shipping and any sales tax. Buyers pay a separate marketplace fee of up to 5% + $1, and boosted listings cost the seller 12% of the sale price. GOAT fee calculatorGOAT deducts a 9.5% commission from the listing price (higher if your seller rating drops below 90) plus a flat seller fee of $5 for prepaid shipping or $0 for drop-off in the US. Cashing out to a bank or PayPal costs another 2.9% of your earnings. Grailed fee calculatorGrailed takes 9% commission on sales of $120 or more and 6% (minimum $1.99) on sales under $120, plus a payment processing fee of 3.49% + $0.49 for US buyers (4.99% + $0.49 international). With a Grailed Label, commission is on the item price only; otherwise it includes shipping. Mercari fee calculatorSince January 6, 2025 Mercari charges sellers a flat 10% of the item price plus buyer-paid shipping, with no separate payment processing fee. Buyers pay a 3.6% Buyer Protection fee on top of the price. Direct deposit is free; Instant Pay costs $3. Poshmark fee calculatorPoshmark keeps a flat $2.95 on sales under $15 and 20% of the sale price on sales of $15 or more. The buyer pays shipping and sales tax, so neither changes your fee. StockX fee calculatorStockX charges a transaction fee of 9% (Level 1) down to 7% (Level 5) of the final sale price, with a $5 minimum, plus a 3% payment processing fee. The buyer pays shipping, so it does not affect your fee. Vestiaire Collective fee calculatorUS sellers pay a 12% selling fee on items priced $83 to $16,667, a flat $10 under $83, and a flat $2,000 above $16,667, plus a 3% payment processing fee with a $3 minimum. Both come off the final sale price; the buyer pays shipping. Vinted fee calculatorVinted charges US sellers nothing to list or sell: you keep 100% of the item price. The buyer pays shipping plus a Buyer Protection fee of $0.70 + 5% of the item price, which is added on top of your price and never comes out of your payout. Whatnot fee calculatorWhatnot charges an 8% commission on the item sale price for most categories (4% for coins), falling as low as 3.5%–6.5% once your 28-day sales pass $15,000, plus a 2.9% + $0.30 payment processing fee on the full order total including buyer-paid shipping and tax.
