How Society6 fees work

Society6 is a print-on-demand marketplace for art and home decor. Artists upload designs, and Society6 prints them on framed art, posters, pillows, wall tapestries and other products, sets the retail price and sells them to customers. Society6 now limits and approves which artists and designs can be sold, so new sellers apply through its Sell Your Art page.

Artist pay is a fixed share of the sale. Since March 18, 2025, Society6 sets standardized retail prices and artists no longer choose their own markups. You earn 10% of the net sale amount on pillows (rectangle and square), wall tapestries, framed art (art prints, framed art prints and recessed framed prints) and posters, and 5% of the net sale amount on every other product. Trade, wholesale and marketplace orders pay 5%. The net sale amount is the customer's final purchase price for the item after any sale or discount.

There are no other deductions to model. Society6 stopped deducting artist shipping fees from earnings for orders placed from March 18, 2025, and it no longer charges for Artist Plans. On a $40.00 framed art print you earn $4.00 and Society6 keeps $36.00.

Earnings are paid by PayPal between the first and third business day of each month, once an order has cleared its 30-day pending period.

  • 10% products: you earn 10% of the net sale amount on pillows (rectangle and square), wall tapestries, framed art (art print, framed art print and recessed framed print) and posters. Society6 keeps the other 90%.
  • All other products: you earn 5% of the net sale amount, and Society6 keeps 95%.
  • Trade, wholesale and marketplace orders: paid at a 5% net sale commission rate.
  • Net sale amount: the customer's final purchase price for the item after any sales or discounts are applied. When Society6 runs a promotion, your earnings fall with the discounted price.
  • Pricing: Society6 standardized retail prices on March 18, 2025. Artists no longer set a markup, so the product type is the only thing that changes your rate.
  • Shipping and plan fees: artist shipping fees are no longer deducted from earnings for orders placed from March 18, 2025, and Society6 no longer charges for Artist Plans.
  • Payout: earnings show as pending for 30 days after the order ships, then are paid by PayPal between the first and third business day of the month. Society6's example: an item that ships on January 15 is paid between March 1 and 3.

Ways to keep more of each Society6 sale

  • Put your best designs on the 10% products first. A $40.00 framed print earns $4.00, while $40.00 of products in the 5% group earns $2.00.
  • Expect lower earnings during sales. Your percentage applies to the price after the discount, so a 30%-off promotion turns a $4.00 royalty on a $40.00 print into $2.80.
  • Plan for a long payout lag. Earnings clear 30 days after shipment and are paid at the start of the following month, so a mid-January sale arrives in early March.
  • Keep your PayPal email current and verified. Society6 pays only through PayPal, and a payment left unclaimed for 30 days is returned.
  • Do not count an order until it clears. Earnings can be cancelled any time before the PayPal payment is processed, for example when the customer gets a full refund.

Society6 fee FAQ

How much do artists make on Society6?

10% of the net sale amount on pillows, wall tapestries, framed art and posters, and 5% on all other products. Trade, wholesale and marketplace orders pay 5%. On a $40.00 framed print that is $4.00.

What percentage does Society6 keep?

90% of the net sale amount on the 10% products (pillows, wall tapestries, framed art and posters) and 95% on everything else, including trade, wholesale and marketplace orders.

Can I set my own prices or markup on Society6?

No. Since March 18, 2025, Society6 uses standardized pricing and artists earn a fixed 5% or 10% of the net sale amount instead of a markup they choose.

Does Society6 charge artists for shipping?

Not any more. Beginning with orders placed March 18, 2025, artist shipping fees are no longer deducted from artist earnings.

Is there a monthly fee to sell on Society6?

Society6's help center says artists are no longer charged for Artist Plans. Billing stopped on a February 3 cutoff and prepaid plan fees were prorated and refunded.

When does Society6 pay artists?

Between the first and third business day of each month, by PayPal, for orders that have finished the 30-day pending period after shipping. Depending on when the order was placed, that is 30 to 60 days after it ships.

Are Society6 earnings based on the full price or the sale price?

The sale price. The net sale amount is the customer's final purchase price for the item after any sales or discounts are applied.
