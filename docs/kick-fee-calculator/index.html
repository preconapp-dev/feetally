How Kick fees work

Kick is a live-streaming platform built to compete with Twitch, and its main pitch to streamers is the split. Kick says you keep 95% of all revenue you receive and the other 5% is taken as a processing fee. That applies to subscriptions and to KICKs, the virtual gifts viewers send in chat.

Subscriptions on Kick are a flat $5 per month. When a viewer subscribes, you receive $4.75 and Kick keeps $0.25. Ten subs pay $47.50; a hundred pay $475. On Twitch's standard 50/50 split the same hundred subs would pay you half of the net revenue instead.

KICKs work the same way: when a viewer sends KICKs on your channel, you receive 95% of the value.

Getting paid runs through Stripe. Kick delivers payouts directly to your bank account; verified streamers can choose weekly or monthly payouts, while non-verified streamers are paid monthly. US bank transfers arrive in 2 to 3 business days and US instant debit-card payouts arrive in minutes, with a $9,000 per-payout cap. Stripe processing fees apply where Stripe charges them, but Kick absorbs Stripe's 1.50% currency conversion fee, so international streamers receive their full payout at real-time rates.

  • Subscriptions: a flat $5 per month. You receive $4.75 (95%) and Kick keeps $0.25 (5%) as a processing fee, per Kick's own example.
  • KICKs (virtual gifts): you receive 95% of the value of KICKs sent on your channel; Kick keeps 5%.
  • Payout method: Kick uses Stripe to deliver payouts directly to your bank account. PayPal is available to viewers for paying subscriptions but not for streamer payouts.
  • Payout schedule: verified streamers choose weekly or monthly payouts; non-verified streamers are paid monthly. US ACH transfers take 2 to 3 business days, US instant debit-card payouts arrive in minutes (capped at $9,000 per payout), and international transfers take 3 to 7 business days.
  • Payout fees: Kick says Stripe processing fees apply where Stripe charges them. For currency conversion, Stripe's 1.50% fee is absorbed by Kick, so you receive the full converted amount.
  • No monthly or listing fees: the 5% is the only cost Kick's help center describes for subs and KICKs.

Ways to keep more of each Kick sale

  • Count subs at $4.75 each when you set goals. 100 subs is $475 a month before any Stripe payout charges; 1,000 subs is $4,750.
  • Get verified if you want weekly money. Verified streamers can switch to weekly payouts; everyone else waits for the monthly cycle.
  • Use ACH rather than instant debit unless you need the cash now. Both are delivered through Stripe, and instant payouts are capped at $9,000 per payout.
  • Promote KICKs for one-off support. They pay the same 95% as subs, so a viewer who will not commit to $5 a month can still send you nearly all of what they spend.
  • Do not price around conversion fees. If you are paid in a non-USD currency, Kick absorbs Stripe's 1.50% conversion fee, so your payout is converted at real-time rates with nothing taken off.

Kick fee FAQ

How much does Kick take from subs?

5%. Subscriptions are $5 a month, you receive $4.75, and Kick keeps $0.25 as a processing fee.

Is the Kick split really 95/5?

Yes. Kick's help center says you keep 95% of all revenue you receive and the other 5% is taken as a processing fee, for both subscriptions and KICKs.

How much is a Kick sub worth to a streamer?

$4.75 per month per subscriber. Ten subs pay $47.50 and a hundred pay $475.

When does Kick pay streamers?

Verified streamers choose weekly or monthly payouts; non-verified streamers are paid monthly. Payouts go through Stripe to your bank: 2 to 3 business days by US ACH, minutes by US instant debit card (up to $9,000 per payout), and 3 to 7 business days internationally.

Does Kick charge payout or currency conversion fees?

Kick absorbs Stripe's 1.50% currency conversion fee, so international streamers get the full converted amount. Stripe processing fees apply only where Stripe charges them.

Can I get paid to PayPal on Kick?

No. PayPal is only available for viewers paying subscriptions; streamer payouts are delivered by Stripe to a bank account.

Good to know

Kick does not publish a minimum payout threshold on its payout article. Gifted subs are not described separately; the calculator assumes they follow the same 95/5 split.
