How Patreon fees work

Patreon is a membership platform: fans pay a monthly (or per-post) amount for access to your posts, community, and perks, and Patreon bills them, delivers the content, and pays you. It is used by podcasters, YouTubers, musicians, writers, artists, and anyone with an audience willing to pay a few dollars a month.

Patreon's fee has two layers. The platform fee is 10% of the income you earn if you published your creator page after August 4, 2025. Creators who launched before that date keep their legacy rate as long as the page stays published: Pro at 8%, Pro + Merch at 11%, or Founders at 5% for pages from before May 7, 2019 that never changed plan. Unpublishing a legacy page moves it to the standard 10%.

The second layer is payment processing. On the standard plan it is 2.9% + $0.30 per payment for cards, Apple Pay, and US PayPal or Venmo (3.9% + $0.30 for PayPal or Venmo from outside the US). Legacy plans pay 5% + $0.10 on tiers priced at $3 or less; above that, Pro and Pro + Merch pay 2.9% + $0.30 and Founders paid out in USD pay 1.6% + $0.30 on card payments. A 2.5% currency conversion fee applies when a member pays in a currency other than your payout currency.

A $5 pledge on the standard plan nets $4.05: $0.50 platform fee and $0.45 processing. Payout fees (25 cents for US direct deposit) come out when you withdraw, not per pledge.

  • Platform fee: 10% of the income you earn on Patreon for creators who published their page after August 4, 2025. Legacy plans: Founders 5% (joined before May 7, 2019, never changed plan), Pro 8%, Pro + Merch 11%. Legacy rates survive only while the page stays continuously published.
  • Payment processing, standard plan: 2.9% + $0.30 per payment for credit cards, Apple Pay, and US PayPal or Venmo, at any payment amount. PayPal or Venmo payments from outside the US are 3.9% + $0.30.
  • Payment processing, legacy plans: tiers priced at $3 or less use micropayment rates of 5% + $0.10 per payment (6% + $0.10 for non-US PayPal/Venmo). Above $3, Pro and Pro + Merch pay 2.9% + $0.30; Founders paid out in USD pay 1.6% + $0.30 by card or Apple Pay and 2.65% + $0.28 by PayPal or Venmo. A Founders creator who changes payout currency keeps the 5% platform fee but moves to standard processing rates.
  • Currency conversion: 2.5% of any payment made in a currency different from your payout currency. A US creator paid in USD owes this only on members who pay in another currency.
  • Payout fees, charged per withdrawal rather than per pledge: $0.25 per payout by US direct deposit; 1% for PayPal (minimum $0.25, capped at $20); $1.00 per payout to Payoneer.
  • One-time purchases (digital products, commissions) carry the same platform and processing fees as memberships. Memberships bought inside the iOS app through Apple's in-app purchase system carry Apple's 30% App Store fee.
  • Your balance already reflects the platform fee, processing fees, and currency conversion. Sales tax and VAT that Patreon adds at checkout are paid by the member and are not part of your fee base.

Ways to keep more of each Patreon sale

  • Keep a legacy page published. A Pro creator pays 8% instead of 10%; on $2,000 a month that is $40 saved every month, and unpublishing the page forfeits the rate for good.
  • On legacy plans, price tiers above $3. A $3 tier pays 5% + $0.10 in processing ($0.25); a $4 tier on Pro pays 2.9% + $0.30 ($0.42) but brings in a dollar more. On the standard plan there is no micropayment rate, so a $1 tier still costs $0.30 + 2.9% plus the 10% fee.
  • Offer annual memberships. One yearly payment carries one $0.30 fixed processing charge instead of twelve, which matters most on $3 to $5 tiers.
  • Choose direct deposit for payouts. It costs $0.25 per payout versus 1% (up to $20) for PayPal; on a $1,500 payout that is $0.25 versus $15.
  • Point iOS members to your Patreon page in a browser. Memberships purchased through Apple in-app purchase carry Apple's 30% App Store fee on top of Patreon's.

Patreon fee FAQ

How much does Patreon take from $5?

On the standard 10% plan, $0.95: a $0.50 platform fee plus $0.45 in processing (2.9% + $0.30). You keep $4.05. A legacy Pro creator pays $0.40 + $0.45 = $0.85 and keeps $4.15. A Founders creator pays $0.25 + $0.38 (1.6% + $0.30 by card) = $0.63 and keeps $4.37.

What percentage does Patreon take in 2026?

10% of earnings for creators who published after August 4, 2025, plus 2.9% + $0.30 payment processing per payment. Creators who launched earlier keep 5%, 8%, or 11% legacy rates while their page stays published.

Does Patreon charge a monthly fee?

No. Patreon takes a percentage of what you earn plus per-payment processing; there is no subscription or setup fee for creators.

How much does Patreon take from a $1 pledge?

On the standard plan, $0.10 platform fee plus $0.33 processing (2.9% + $0.30), so about $0.43 of the dollar. Legacy plans use the $3-and-under micropayment rate: $0.05 + $0.10 = $0.15 processing plus the plan percentage.

What are Patreon's payout fees?

$0.25 per payout by direct deposit for US creators, 1% (minimum $0.25, capped at $20) for PayPal, and $1.00 per payout to Payoneer. These are charged when you withdraw, not on each pledge.

Does Patreon charge a currency conversion fee?

Yes, 2.5% on any payment made in a currency different from your payout currency. Members paying in USD to a USD-payout creator are not charged it.

Does Patreon take 30% on iPhone memberships?

Memberships purchased through Apple's in-app purchase system in the iOS app carry Apple's 30% App Store fee, according to Patreon's fee article. Memberships started on the web do not.

Good to know

Rates shown are for USD payouts and card payments. Non-US PayPal/Venmo payments are 3.9% + $0.30 (6% + $0.10 for legacy micropayments). Founders processing follows the fee article's table of Founders payment processing rates (USD only), which lists one standard rate: 1.6% + $0.30 for cards and Apple Pay and 2.65% + $0.28 for PayPal or Venmo. The article says Founders tiers of $3 or less use micropayment rates without listing a separate Founders figure, so the calculator applies the legacy 5% + $0.10 there; the PayPal/Venmo rate is not modeled. Non-USD processing and payout rates differ by currency; see the fee article. Patreon says Apple's in-app purchase system began rolling out for one-time purchases in January 2024 and for new memberships in November 2024. Payout fees and Apple's fee are not modeled per pledge.
