How Stripe fees work

Stripe is the payment processor behind a large share of online checkouts, subscription apps and marketplaces. If you sell through your own website, take card payments in an app, bill clients with Stripe Invoicing or run a Stripe Terminal reader at the counter, Stripe takes a cut of every successful payment. There is no monthly fee and no setup fee on standard pricing; you pay only when you get paid.

The headline rate for a US business is 2.9% + 30¢ per successful online card payment with a domestic card. In-person payments through Stripe Terminal cost 2.7% + 5¢. Two surcharges stack on top of the card rate: an extra 0.5% when you type a card number in manually, and an extra 1.5% when the card was issued outside the United States. If Stripe has to convert the payment into US dollars, add another 1%.

ACH Direct Debit is the cheap option for large invoices: 0.8% of the amount, capped at $5.00. On a $2,000 invoice that is $5 instead of $58.30 by card. This calculator applies Stripe's published pay-as-you-go rates; if you negotiate custom pricing with Stripe, your numbers will differ.

  • Online card payments: 2.9% + 30¢ per successful transaction with a US-issued card. A $100 payment costs $3.20.
  • In person with Stripe Terminal: 2.7% + 5¢ per successful transaction. A $100 tap or dip costs $2.75.
  • Manually entered cards: an extra 0.5% on top of the online rate, so 3.4% + 30¢ in total when you key a card number into the Dashboard or a virtual terminal.
  • International cards: an extra 1.5% whenever the card was issued outside the US. Stripe lists this for both online and Terminal payments.
  • Currency conversion: an extra 1% when Stripe converts the payment into your settlement currency.
  • ACH Direct Debit: 0.8% of the payment, capped at $5.00. The cap kicks in at $625, so anything larger costs a flat $5.
  • Disputes: $15.00 for each dispute you receive, and another $15.00 if you counter it manually. Instant Payouts cost 1.5% of the payout with a 50¢ minimum; standard payouts are free.

Ways to keep more of each Stripe sale

  • Offer ACH Direct Debit on invoices over a few hundred dollars. A $1,000 invoice costs $5 by ACH versus $29.30 by card.
  • Avoid keying card numbers by hand. Send a Stripe payment link or invoice instead and you skip the 0.5% manual-entry surcharge.
  • Price and settle in USD when you can. Letting Stripe convert currency adds 1% on top of the 1.5% international-card fee, so a foreign order can cost 5.4% + 30¢.
  • Use Stripe Terminal for walk-in customers. 2.7% + 5¢ beats the online rate on every ticket, and the gap is widest on small amounts.
  • Turn on Link for returning customers. Link card payments cost the same 2.9% + 30¢, and Link instant bank payments are 2.6% + 30¢.

Stripe fee FAQ

How much does Stripe take from $100?

$3.20 for an online payment with a US card (2.9% + 30¢), leaving you $96.80. In person through Terminal it is $2.75. Online with an international card it is $4.70.

Does Stripe charge a monthly fee?

No. Stripe's standard pricing has no setup or monthly fees; you pay per successful transaction. Add-on products such as Sigma, Billing and Tax have their own monthly prices.

What is Stripe's fee for ACH payments?

0.8% of the payment, capped at $5.00. A $500 ACH debit costs $4; anything of $625 or more costs $5.

How much does Stripe charge for international cards?

An extra 1.5% on top of the base rate, plus 1% more if currency conversion is needed. A $100 online payment with a non-US card costs $4.70, or $5.70 with conversion.

What does Stripe charge for a chargeback?

$15.00 for each dispute you receive, and a further $15.00 if you respond to it manually.

How much is a Stripe instant payout?

1.5% of the payout amount with a 50¢ minimum. Standard payouts to your bank account are free.

Is Stripe cheaper than PayPal?

For online payments, yes: Stripe charges 2.9% + 30¢ versus PayPal's 3.49% + 49¢ for PayPal Checkout. PayPal's standard card rate of 2.99% + 49¢ is closer, but still 28¢ more on a $100 sale.

Good to know

Rates are Stripe's published US pay-as-you-go pricing; high-volume businesses negotiate custom pricing. The 0.5% manual-entry surcharge and the 1% currency-conversion surcharge are listed under online card payments; Terminal lists the 1.5% international surcharge. Link card payments cost the same as regular cards; Link instant bank payments are 2.6% + 30¢ and stablecoin payments 1.5%. Standard payouts are free, but the pricing page does not state payout timing, which depends on your account's payout schedule. Dispute fees ($15 received, $15 countered) are not modeled in the calculator.
