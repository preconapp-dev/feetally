How TikTok Shop fees work

TikTok Shop is the in-app store built into TikTok. Products sell through shoppable videos, live streams, the Shop tab and creator affiliate content, and the buyer never leaves the app. Sellers are brands, dropshippers and small businesses; you need a US business or individual seller account, and TikTok can also store and ship inventory through Fulfilled by TikTok.

The fee structure is unusually simple. TikTok Shop charges a referral fee of 6% on most categories, calculated on what the buyer paid plus any TikTok-funded discount, minus sales tax. Fine jewelry (diamond, gold, jade, platinum, and ruby, sapphire or emerald pieces) and the Pre-Owned categories pay 5%. There is no listing fee, no monthly fee and no separate payment processing fee.

The cost that changes the math is affiliate commission. Most TikTok Shop sales come through creators, and you decide what commission to pay them, commonly 10% to 20% of the product revenue, on top of the referral fee. TikTok also applies sales tax to the referral fee itself for sellers registered in Texas and West Virginia since November 1, 2025. The referral fee has moved several times: it rose to 6% on April 1, 2024, and TikTok periodically runs lower promotional rates for new sellers, so check the current offer in Seller Center before you plan margins.

  • Referral fee: 6% in most categories, including fashion, beauty, home, electronics, food, toys, sports, pet supplies and books. TikTok's formula is Referral Fee Rate × (Customer Payment + Platform Discount − Tax), charged on every completed order, and TikTok states the base includes the shipping fee paid by the customer. A discount TikTok funds is added back before the fee is calculated; one you fund is not.
  • Reduced categories: 5% on fine jewelry (diamond, gold, jade, platinum or carat gold, ruby, sapphire, emerald) since October 31, 2024, and 5% on the Pre-Owned categories: bags, watches, footwear, menswear, womenswear, fashion accessories, luggage, refurbished phones and electronics, and pre-owned collectible trading cards, coins, figures and comic books. New items in the Collectibles categories stay at 6%. In both Pre-Owned and Collectibles, any portion of a sale over $10,000 is charged 3%.
  • Affiliate commission (optional): when a creator's video, live stream or showcase link produces the sale, you pay the commission rate you set on that product. TikTok calculates it as (Revenue − Refunds) × Commission rate and pays the creator after the order settles, typically 15 days after delivery.
  • No separate processing, listing or subscription fees: TikTok states the referral fee policy covers all TikTok Shop fees other than shipping and tax. Shop Ads, Fulfilled by TikTok (FBT) storage and fulfillment, and platform shipping programs are separate optional costs.
  • Refunds: the referral fee is charged only on completed orders, and a partial refund reduces the fee base. TikTok's own example: an order with $102 paid, $5 platform discount and $2 tax pays 6% × $105 = $6.30; after a $51 partial refund the fee falls to $3.15.
  • Refund administration fee: when an order is refunded, returned or canceled after it was placed, TikTok keeps a Refund Administration Fee of 20% of the referral fee on the refunded part, capped at $5 per SKU since May 15, 2025. In TikTok's example that is $0.63 on the half-refunded order and $1.26 if the whole order is refunded. Refundable creator samples and orders auto-canceled before shipping are exempt.
  • Sales tax on the fee: since November 1, 2025, TikTok Shop adds sales tax to the referral fee on the seller invoice for sellers registered in West Virginia or Texas. No action is needed; it shows in settlement reports.

Ways to keep more of each TikTok Shop sale

  • Treat affiliate commission as the real fee. At 6% referral plus a 15% commission, a $30 sale nets $23.70 before your costs, so price affiliate-driven products with at least 25% headroom.
  • Set commission per product, not shop-wide. Offer higher rates on high-margin items and keep low-margin staples at the minimum, since commission is only paid on affiliate-attributed orders.
  • Discounts you fund lower the referral fee base; discounts TikTok funds do not. A $5 seller coupon on a $30 item saves you $0.30 in referral fee on top of the $5 you gave away.
  • Watch the new-seller promotions. TikTok has run reduced referral fee windows for newly onboarded shops; the exact rate and length change by quarter and are listed in Seller Center when you sign up.
  • Check your settlement reports for the sales tax line if you are registered in Texas or West Virginia; the tax on the referral fee is small but it is deducted from your payout.

TikTok Shop fee FAQ

What is the TikTok Shop referral fee in 2026?

6% of the amount the buyer paid (plus any platform-funded discount, minus sales tax) in most categories. Fine jewelry and the Pre-Owned categories pay 5%. The 6% rate has applied since April 1, 2024.

Does TikTok Shop charge a payment processing fee?

No separate one. TikTok's referral fee policy states it covers all TikTok Shop fees except shipping and tax, so there is no PayPal-style percentage plus fixed fee on top.

Is there a monthly or listing fee on TikTok Shop?

No. Opening a shop and listing products is free. You pay the referral fee only when an order completes, plus any affiliate commission or ad spend you choose.

How much do I pay creators?

Whatever commission rate you set for the product in the Affiliate Center, applied to the product revenue on orders that came through that creator's content. It is paid on top of the 6% referral fee and only on affiliate-attributed orders.

When does TikTok Shop pay sellers?

After the order is completed, which is after delivery plus the return window. The referral fee and any affiliate commission are deducted in the settlement, and creators receive their commission after your order settles, typically 15 days after delivery.

Does the referral fee apply to sales tax or shipping?

Not to tax: tax is subtracted before the rate is applied. Shipping is different: TikTok states the fee base includes the shipping fee paid by the customer. Separately, since November 1, 2025, TikTok adds sales tax to the referral fee itself for sellers registered in Texas or West Virginia.

Did TikTok Shop raise its fees?

Yes. The US referral fee increased to 6% on April 1, 2024, and TikTok says sellers on promotional plans stay on them until the promotion ends. TikTok lowered fine jewelry to 5% on October 31, 2024.

Good to know

Rates are from TikTok Shop Academy (US) pages read 2026-10-10; the category rate page is dated 10/08/2026 and still lists 6% for most categories. TikTok defines the fee base as the amount the buyer paid plus platform-funded discounts minus tax, and the category page states that the base includes the shipping fee paid by the customer, so this calculator includes shipping. The 5% rate covers the Pre-Owned categories only; new items in the Collectibles categories are listed at 6%. The Refund Administration Fee (20% of the referral fee on refunded orders, capped at $5 per SKU) is not modeled. Affiliate commission is modeled on the item price; TikTok's page defines it as (Revenue − Refunds) × rate without stating whether shipping is in revenue. The 3% rate on the portion of a pre-owned or collectibles sale over $10,000 is not modeled. TikTok's 2025 new-seller referral fee promotion pages were not reachable when checked, so no promotional rate is stated here. Some third-party guides quote other US rates (for example 8%); TikTok's own pages do not. FBT fulfillment, Shop Ads and platform shipping costs are not modeled.
