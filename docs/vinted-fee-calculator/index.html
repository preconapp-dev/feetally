How Vinted fees work

Vinted is a secondhand marketplace for clothing, shoes, accessories, kids' items, and some home goods. Its US pitch is simple: selling is free. Most US sellers are people clearing out closets, alongside a growing number of resellers who prefer a 0% fee to Poshmark's 20% or eBay's roughly 13%.

There is no listing fee, no selling fee, and no payment processing fee for sellers. If your item sells for $25, $25 lands in your Vinted balance. Shipping is paid by the buyer, who chooses a carrier option at checkout; you print the prepaid label and drop the package off.

Vinted funds itself on the buyer side. Every order carries a mandatory Buyer Protection fee of $0.70 plus 5% of the item price, added automatically at checkout on top of your price, shipping, and any sales tax. On a $25 item that is $1.95. It covers refunds if an item never arrives or is not as described.

Because buyers see your price plus that fee plus shipping, a $25 listing might show as roughly $32 to $35 delivered. Keep that in mind when you price against other apps, and remember that the only optional costs on your side are paid promotions: item bumps and Wardrobe Spotlight, priced in the app before you confirm.

  • Selling fee: 0%. Vinted's US pricelist has no listing fee, no commission, and no payment processing fee for sellers. What the buyer pays for the item is what you receive.
  • Buyer Protection fee (paid by the buyer): $0.70 plus 5% of the item price, or of the combined bundle price, added automatically to every order at checkout. It does not touch your payout.
  • Shipping: paid by the buyer, who picks a delivery option at checkout. You get a prepaid label to print. There is no fee to you for the label.
  • Sales tax: collected from the buyer where required and handled by Vinted; it is not deducted from your price.
  • Optional promotions: item bumps (3 or 7 days) and Wardrobe Spotlight are the only seller-paid features. The price is shown in the app before you confirm each order and is not a percentage of sales.
  • Payout: proceeds go to your Vinted balance after the buyer confirms everything is OK (or the inspection window passes), and you can transfer the balance to your bank account.

Ways to keep more of each Vinted sale

  • Price for the buyer's real total. A $25 item costs the buyer $26.95 before shipping because of the $0.70 + 5% protection fee, so match competitors on the delivered price, not the sticker.
  • Accept offers freely. With no percentage fee, every dollar you knock off is exactly one dollar less in your pocket, and nothing is lost to commission math.
  • Bundles help buyers more than you might expect: the $0.70 fixed part of the protection fee and shipping are paid once for the whole bundle, so a bundle discount can still leave the buyer paying less per item.
  • Compare before you cross-list. The same $25 sale nets $25.00 on Vinted, $20.00 on Poshmark, and $23.72 on Depop after its 3.3% + $0.45 processing fee.
  • Use bumps only for items with proven demand. A bump is a flat charge you pay whether or not the item sells, so it is the one way to lose money on a fee-free platform.

Vinted fee FAQ

Does Vinted charge sellers a fee?

No. US sellers pay no listing fee, no selling fee, and no payment processing fee. You keep 100% of the item price.

What is the Vinted Buyer Protection fee?

A mandatory fee the buyer pays at checkout: $0.70 plus 5% of the item price. It is added on top of the seller's price and does not reduce the seller's payout.

Who pays shipping on Vinted?

The buyer. They choose a shipping option at checkout and pay for it; the seller prints a prepaid label.

How much does Vinted take from a $50 sale?

Nothing. You receive $50.00. The buyer pays $50 plus a $3.20 protection fee ($0.70 + 5%), plus shipping and any sales tax.

Why is my Vinted listing price different from what buyers see?

Buyers see your price plus the Buyer Protection fee, shipping, and any sales tax. A $25 item shows as $26.95 before shipping.

Is there a fee to withdraw money from Vinted?

Vinted's US pricelist does not list a withdrawal fee. Proceeds sit in your Vinted balance and can be transferred to your bank account; check the app for the current transfer timing.

Does Vinted charge sales tax to sellers?

No. Where sales tax applies, Vinted collects it from the buyer at checkout as a marketplace facilitator. It is separate from your price.

Good to know

Fee figures come from Vinted's US pricelist (stated as effective July 1, 2024) and the Buyer Protection fee help article, which describes the fee as 'usually 5% + $0.70'; certain categories (for example electronics) may carry a different buyer fee. Bump and Wardrobe Spotlight prices vary and are shown in the app before purchase, so they are not modeled. Payout timing and bank-transfer details were not verified on an official page for this update. Fees differ in other Vinted countries.
