How to read this table

"You keep" is the sale price plus any shipping you charged, minus every seller-side fee at that platform's default settings: no store subscription, the most common category, standard payment method, no optional ads. Platforms whose fees depend heavily on a plan or category (Amazon, Shopify, StockX) can look better or worse than this once you pick your own options, so treat the table as a first cut and open the calculator that matters to you.

Buyer-paid fees (Vinted's buyer protection, Mercari's buyer fee, Airbnb's guest fee) are not subtracted from what you keep, but they raise the buyer's total and can affect how competitive your price is.
